# Lumen Fox AI - Responsible AI Policy (SYNTHETIC)

- AI usage policy approved by the executive team; AI governance committee reviews new use cases monthly.
- AI risk assessment (ISO 42001 aligned) and a DPIA for any use case touching personal data.
- Human-in-the-loop approval for consequential actions; dual control for payments; kill switch per agent.
- Agent inventory with agent owner and model card; vendor assessment and DPA with zero data retention for every provider.
