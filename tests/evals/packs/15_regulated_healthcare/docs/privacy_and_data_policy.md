# Meadowlark Health Partners - Privacy and Data Protection Policy (SYNTHETIC)

- Compliance obligations: HIPAA, GDPR for EU research partners, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 reports from vendors.
- Data classification: restricted (patient records, PHI/PII), confidential, internal, public; data residency in-country.
- Backup policy: nightly, retention period 7 years for records, snapshot every 4 hours; archive to immutable storage.
- Data inventory with a data owner and data steward per system; data catalogue in the governance tool with metadata.
- Every new system or AI use case gets a DPIA (impact assessment) and a risk assessment before go-live.
- Vendor assessment and a data processing agreement (DPA) with every sub-processor; third-party risk reviewed yearly.
